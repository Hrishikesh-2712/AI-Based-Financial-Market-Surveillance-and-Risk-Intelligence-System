INTRODUCTION

What this system does

The system monitors Bank Nifty market data on an ongoing basis and applies machine learning to detect statistically and temporally unusual behaviour in price and volume — the kind of activity that manual, rule-based surveillance tends to miss or flag too late.

Detected anomalies are combined with a news-analysis layer, scored through a risk engine, and surfaced through a live dashboard and a retrieval-augmented (RAG) chatbot for querying the system in natural language.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Traditional, threshold-based market surveillance struggles to adapt to new patterns of unusual activity, and typically produces alerts without the underlying reasoning an analyst needs to act on them quickly.

This project explores whether a learned, explainable approach — combining anomaly detection models with contextual news signals — can surface risk earlier and with more usable context than static rules alone.

OBJECTIVES

What the project set out to build

01

Detect anomalies in Bank Nifty price and volume data using Isolation Forest and Temporal Convolutional Networks.

02

Incorporate news context so that detected anomalies can be related to concurrent market news and events.

03

Produce a composite risk score through a dedicated risk engine that combines model outputs into a single, interpretable signal.

04

Serve results in real time through a FastAPI backend to a live dashboard built with Streamlit and Plotly.

05

Enable natural-language querying of system output through a RAG-based chatbot using LangChain, FAISS, and Gemini.

06

Track experiments and model iterations systematically using MLflow.

METHODOLOGY

The processing pipeline

Data flows through nine stages, from raw market data to a queryable risk output.

1

Data Collection

Bank Nifty market data retrieved via the FYERS API.

2

Preprocessing

Cleaning, normalizing, and structuring raw market data for modeling.

3

Feature Engineering

Deriving the price, volume, and volatility features the models are trained on.

4

Isolation Forest

Unsupervised detection of statistical outliers in the feature set.

5

TCN

A Temporal Convolutional Network models sequential patterns across time.

6

News Analysis

Concurrent financial news is analyzed and linked to detected anomalies.

7

Risk Engine

Model outputs and news signals are combined into a composite risk score.

8

Dashboard

Risk scores and anomalies are served live through a Streamlit + Plotly dashboard.

9

RAG Chatbot

A LangChain + FAISS + Gemini chatbot lets users query the system in natural language.
